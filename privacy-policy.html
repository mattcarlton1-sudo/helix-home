Privacy Policy

Helix Home app · Helix Integration, Wentzville, Missouri · Effective October 25, 2026

Helix Home ("the app") lets you control a Helix home hub installed by Helix Integration. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Helix Integration does not run any server that the app reports to.

Information kept on your device

  • Saved hubs: the names and network addresses of the hubs you add. These are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall it.

Communication with your hub

The app connects directly to the Helix hub address you enter, either on your home Wi-Fi or through your own private Tailscale network. Lighting, camera, music, climate and other home data travels only between the app and the hub you own. It is never sent to Helix Integration.

Permissions

  • Local Network: used to find and connect to your hub on your Wi-Fi.
  • Camera: used only when you tap "Scan QR code" to read the QR code on your hub. Images are processed on the device and are not saved or sent anywhere.

Demo mode

"Try demo home" uses sample data built into the app and makes no network connections.

Third-party services

Some features may open or load content from other services when you use them. For example, the Music section can open the WiiM Home app or its App Store page, and a car-location map loads map images from OpenStreetMap. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
