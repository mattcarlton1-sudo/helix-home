Getting started

  1. Connect your iPhone or iPad to your home Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Helix Home and allow Local Network access when asked.
  3. Tap Find hub on this Wi-Fi, scan the QR code on your hub, or type the hub address your installer gave you (for example 192.168.1.20:8080).
  4. Tap Save & connect. After that, the app opens straight to Home control.

Common questions

The app can't find my hub. Make sure you're on the same Wi-Fi as the hub. Then check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and confirm Helix is turned on, or enter the address by hand.

Can I control my home while I'm away? Yes, if your installer set up secure remote access with Tailscale. Use the https://…ts.net address they gave you.

How do I switch homes? Tap the small ⬡ button at the bottom-left, then choose Switch hub.

No hub yet? Tap Try demo home to explore a sample house.

Installer mode is for Helix Integration technicians and requires an installer PIN.
